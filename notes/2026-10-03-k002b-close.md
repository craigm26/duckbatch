## Verdict
**k002b fails line 1**, as k002 did. The hypothesis held in direction: doubling the sideways
term moved the angle from 5.0° to 4.4°, with distance and falls still inside their lines. It did
not reach the line. As the design said, there is no k002c here: the left kick is not offered as a
better left kick.

## What it shows
- The left kick's sideways error responds to the sideways weight with diminishing returns
  (7.2° → 5.0° at −12 → 4.4° at −24), where the right kick reached 3.4° at −12. The two feet
  are not mirror images in this network.
- The price is small but visible: peak ball speed 1.37 → 1.34 m/s, backlash travel −2%.

## Where it goes instead
k002 and k002b are **contenders in the left-kick duels** (`duckbatch kick-pairs left`): a person
choosing between Pollen's left kick, k002 and k002b is exactly the per-skill RLHF the app now
collects. Whether a person even prefers the straighter kick is the question those picks answer.
