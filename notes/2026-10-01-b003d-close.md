## Why: the stride's sway counts as tracking error
mjlab's tracking error is taken on the instantaneous body velocity, so a walk is charged for its
stride as well as for its mean speed.

**Measured** (`records/s002-b003d-diagnosis/gait_wobble.*`: teacher, student and b003b at 0.20
and 0.30 m/s, CPU, 24 envs):
- Within a stride, the body velocity varies by 0.10–0.15 m/s (std), mostly sideways sway at
  0.09–0.13 m/s. That is more than a 0.10 m/s command.
- Averaged over 0.5 s it falls to 0.03–0.05.

**What the term pays along the way.** The six measured strides were moved to a mean of 0, half
and all of a 0.10 m/s forward command and judged against it. Ranges are over the six; the term
is the average over the stride of exp(−error²/width²).

| width | standing | stepping in place | half the command | the command |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mjlab's, instantaneous | 0.905 | 0.75–0.83 | 0.81–0.89 | 0.84–0.92 |
| b003d's, instantaneous | 0.20 | 0.09–0.17 | 0.25–0.45 | 0.38–0.64 |
| b003d's, on the 0.5 s average | 0.20 | 0.21–0.25 | 0.57–0.64 | 0.81–0.93 |

- **Under mjlab's width, walking 0.10 m/s exactly earns about what standing still earns**
  (0.84–0.92 against 0.905). A stride at its own mean speed pays what standing pays at a
  0.09–0.13 m/s command. That is
  where every walker's dead band sits, Pollen's velstand included. The pay for walking slowly
  is left to air time (and b003b's progress term), against what stepping costs elsewhere.
- **b003d's width fixed the top but kept a dip at the first step.**
  - Walking the command now out-earns standing by 0.2–0.4 of the term (+0.4–0.9/s). The design
    said +1.6/s because its arithmetic took a slow walk's error to be its mean-speed error; the
    sway takes half to three-quarters of that.
  - Stepping in place, the first move away from standing, pays 0.09–0.17 against standing's
    0.20, so it is downhill. PPO never crossed it.
- **On the 0.5 s average the dip goes.** Stepping in place pays 0.21–0.25 and the command
  0.81–0.93, so the path from standing to walking is uphill all the way.

## Next, in order (not run; each needs a go-ahead)
1. **Judge the stride, not the instant (b003e).** b003d's relative term, on the body velocity
   averaged over 0.5 s and reset at each episode start. One change from b003d; the table's last
   row is its expected tracking pay. Caveats: the average lags a new command by about 0.5 s
   (commands hold 3–8 s), and the policy does not observe it.
2. **Upright anchor off** (`coef_walk: 0`) is still open. Turning in place improved in all three
   runs and linear tracking in none. Turn-in-place frames sit away from both anchored regions
   (standing still, and walking at 0.35 m/s or more); slow walking sits between them. But the
   table explains the band's edge without the anchor.
3. **The angular term is instantaneous too.** It charges walking's roll and pitch −0.3 to −0.6/s
   (s001); the same averaging may be worth trying there later.
4. **b003b stays the best network.**

## Not claimed
Simulation only, in mjlab. The sway figures come from three walkers at 0.20 and 0.30 m/s, and
the table moves their strides to slower means. A real slow gait's sway might be smaller; the
dip and the crossover would move with it.
