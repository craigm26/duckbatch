# p001, env 3: not a lost command, most likely the walkers' dead band

2026-09-24 · from the Pi (no GPU, so no re-run) · follows up duckbatch#2

## What was seen

In p001's recorded clips, env 3 stands still under every moving command for all four policies
(knee spread 0.002-0.032 rad, against 0.10-0.21 walking). The first reading, in duckbatch#2, was
that the pinned twist did not reach that env.

## What the evidence says instead

1. **The command path is clean.** mjlab 1.3.0's `UniformVelocityCommand._resample_command`
   marks an env standing by `uniform() <= rel_standing_envs`. `fixed_command` pins that to 0.0,
   pins the ranges and the standing curriculum before `env.reset(seed)`, and sets resampling to
   1e9 s. VelStand's prone and crouch spawn slices ramp from zero by training step, so a freshly
   built eval env does not use them. Nothing found zeroes one env's command.
2. **The walkers have a low-command dead band.** On the canon plant (duckbench, two-duck scene,
   no randomisation), alpha_walking moved 1.5 cm in 8 s at vx 0.20 and about 0.11 m/s at 0.25.
   p001's commands sit at that edge: fwd_025 is exactly Pollen's forward limit.
3. **Env 3 is not special.** From p001's own per-env features (a policy counts as "stood" when
   its linear error exceeds 80% of the commanded speed):

   | command | envs where all four policies stood |
   |---|---|
   | fwd_015 | 84% |
   | fwd_025 | 30% |
   | turn_06 | 24% (on yaw error) |
   | arc | 84% |

   Every policy shares an env's randomisation (same seed), so an env whose mass, friction or
   noise pushes a command into the dead band does it to all four at once. That is what env 3
   looks like.

## What this means

- p001's forward and turn pairs mostly compare **standing** policies, which is why the
  preference sizing leaned on smoothness (jitter, action rate) more than on walking. The
  close note's finding stands, but its reason is now clear.
- The app already drops slots where no policy stepped (duckbatch#2, `usable_envs`).
- **A re-run should use commands the walkers actually follow.** Pollen's forward limit is 0.25 and
  the band reaches past 0.20, so forward walking is only reliable near the limit. Any re-run needs
  a pre-registration that states the commands and the dead-band check.

## Not yet checked, and how

print `env.command_manager.get_command("twist")[:8]` and the twist term's `is_standing_env[:8]`.
If env 3's row reads (0.25, 0, 0) and False, this note is right. If it doesn't, duckbatch#2's
