## Why, as far as the numbers go
Practice moved into the band and the duck learned to stand there. At a 0.10 m/s command,
mjlab's tracking term (std √0.1) pays exp(−0.01/0.1) ≈ 0.90 of its weight 2.0 for standing
still. That is 1.8 per step, against at most 3.0 for walking it perfectly, and walking risks the
fallen tax and the anchor's pull toward the teacher, which stands near zero. A third of the
free envs now spent their episodes where standing is nearly as good as walking, so PPO took
the safe answer more often. Less practice above the band cost b003b's turning and fast walking.

So the dead band is not short of practice. Standing is too well paid there.

## Next, in order (not run; each needs a go-ahead)
1. **Command-relative tracking:** scale the tracking error by the command, error / max(|cmd|,
   c₀). Standing at 0.10 then pays exp(−1/σ²) instead of 0.9, while fast commands keep
   mjlab's gradient. This targets the cause found here. b003 only narrowed σ everywhere,
   which killed the gradient at speed.
2. **No anchor pull toward standing for small non-zero commands** (`walk_min_speed`'s
   "or ≈0").
3. **Keep b003b as the best network so far:** turning 2.2× the student, 0.4 m/s above the
   teacher, falls unchanged.
