# k001 design: a straighter right kick (pre-registered 2026-10-02)

## Why
Phase 2 of putting better skills on real Microducks. The first target was backlash robustness,
but it was dropped before any GPU time was spent. Measured on CPU (32 envs, seeds 2001 and
2002), Pollen's right kick is not hurt by backlash:

| | travel | peak speed |
|---|---|---|
| plain | 5.07 / 4.91 m | 1.31 / 1.28 m/s |
| backlash | 5.25 / 5.03 m | 1.40 / 1.35 m/s |

Neither variant fell. The kick does go **7.3° off line** on average, 0.66 m wide at the end of a
~5 m roll (seed 2001, plain task). For Duck Soccer shooting on a real duck, aim matters more
than distance. Craig chose "straighter".

## Method
- Pollen's task (Mjlab-BallKick-Flat-MicroDuck) and reward, unchanged, plus one term:
  `ball_lateral_speed`, |ball velocity · perp(kick direction)|, weight −12.0. That is equal and
  opposite to Pollen's `ball_forward_velocity` weight of 12.0, so a kick θ off line loses about
  sin θ of what its forward speed earns. Slowing down does not escape it: the sideways speed
  falls with the forward speed.
- Warm start from Pollen's own `ball_kick_right.onnx` (teachers/, d5a8b55 sha256 147a32c3…).
  Plain PPO with no anchor; init_std 0.2, lr 3e-4, entropy 0.002; 1000 iterations × 2048 envs on
  HF Jobs l4x1.
- Measured with `skill_eval.kick`, which reads each env until its first episode ends:
  - 256 envs, seeds 2001–2003, on the plain task and on the backlash twin;
  - Pollen's kick and the fine-tune compared on the same seeds;
  - the angle is atan2(off-line distance, travel) per env, averaged.

## Pass lines (plain task, mean over seeds 2001–2003)
1. **Straighter:** angle off line ≤ 4.0°. Pollen's kick measured 7.3° on CPU; Pollen's own number
   on this run's seeds is reported beside it.
2. **Not weaker:** ball travel ≥ 0.95 × Pollen's.
3. **Not riskier:**
   - fell share ≤ Pollen's + 0.02;
   - support-foot-down share ≥ Pollen's − 0.05, so no hopping.
4. **Installable:** Pollen's `check_onnx`, `smoke_run_onnx` and `validate_manifest` pass on the
   exported network and its episodic manifest (0.5 s, slot `kick_right`).
- **Reported, not a line:** the same numbers on the backlash twin.

## Expectation to beat
Better than even for line 1. The term gives a direct gradient on the one quantity in question,
and the kick already reaches the ball cleanly. Risk: the policy may trade distance for aim by
nudging the ball, which line 2 guards.

## Not claimed
Simulation only, in mjlab with Pollen's domain randomisation. Nothing here has run on a physical
duck; that is Phase 5, behind its own gate.
