# k002b design: the left kick with the sideways term doubled (pre-registered 2026-10-03)

## Why
k002 took Pollen's left kick from 7.2° to 5.0° off line and missed its 4.0° line
(`notes/2026-10-03-k002-close.md`). Distance, falls and the contract all held. The working
hypothesis is that −12 on sideways ball speed does not charge enough against the forward term
(+12, capped at the 0.25 m/s target) for this network.

## Method
k002 exactly, with one change: `ball_lateral_speed` weight −12 → **−24**. Same task
(Duckbatch-BallKick-Left-Flat-MicroDuck), same warm start (Pollen's `ball_kick_left.onnx`), plain
PPO, init_std 0.2, 1000 × 2048 on l4x1, measured by `skill_eval.kick` on 256 envs × seeds
2001–2003, plain task and backlash twin.

## Pass lines (k002's, unchanged; plain task, mean over the three seeds, against Pollen's left kick)
1. **Straighter:** angle off line ≤ 4.0°.
2. **Not weaker:** travel ≥ 0.95 × Pollen's.
3. **Not riskier:** falls ≤ Pollen's + 0.02; support foot down ≥ Pollen's − 0.05.
4. **Installable:** Pollen's three checks pass, slot `kick_left`.
- **Reported, not a line:** the backlash twin, and k002 beside it.

## What would count against the hypothesis
Angle no better than k002's 5.0°, or a gain paid for with distance (line 2) or the support foot
(line 3). Either is reported as the result; there is no k002c in this design.

## Not claimed
Simulation only. Nothing has run on a physical duck.
