# b003e design: judge the stride, not the instant (pre-registered 2026-10-01)

## Why
b003d's close (`notes/2026-10-01-b003d-close.md`, records `records/s002-b003d-diagnosis/`) found
that the stride's sway is the dead band.
- mjlab's tracking error is taken on the instantaneous body velocity, and a stride sways the body
  0.10–0.15 m/s, mostly sideways. That is more than a 0.10 m/s command.
- Under mjlab's width, walking 0.10 m/s exactly earns about what standing earns (0.84–0.92
  against 0.905).
- b003d's relative width raised the top but left stepping in place, the first move away from
  standing, below standing (0.09–0.17 against 0.20). None of its checkpoints walked at 0.10.
- On the planar velocity averaged over 0.5 s the sway falls to 0.03–0.05 m/s. The measured
  strides then pay 0.21–0.25 for stepping in place and 0.81–0.93 for the command, against 0.20
  for standing, so the path from standing to walking is uphill all the way.

## Method (the one change from b003d)
- **The term:** `src/duckbatch/rewards.py` `track_linear_velocity_stride` is b003d's
  `track_linear_velocity_relative` (same width rule, through one shared helper), on the planar
  body velocity averaged over the last 0.5 s (25 env steps).
  - It is a class term: mjlab builds it with `(cfg, env)` and calls `reset` when episodes end.
  - An env's history is refilled with its current velocity on its first step of an episode, so
    nothing carries over from the last one.
  - Every env pushes once per env step, even when other envs restart that step. A test holds
    this; the first draft did not, and the test was checked to catch it.
  - The vertical part stays instantaneous, at mjlab's width, as in b003d.
- **Everywhere, not only in the band:** the average applies at every command.
  - At and above 0.4 m/s the width is still mjlab's, but the sway no longer counts, so walking
    pays more there too.
  - Stand commands are judged on the average as well.
- **Checked in a real VelStand env** (CPU, 16 envs, all three terms at weight 2;
  `records/s003-stride-term-check/`):

  | policy at command | speed | mjlab | b003d | b003e |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | student at 0.10, which it answers by standing | 0.003 | 1.80/s | 0.41/s | 0.45/s |
  | student at 0.40 | 0.208 | 1.04/s | 1.04/s | 1.27/s |
  | b003b at 0.30 | 0.146 | 1.18/s | 0.85/s | 1.21/s |
  | student at a stand command | 0.010 | 1.86/s | 1.86/s | 1.90/s |

- **Everything else is b003d,** which is b003b plus the relative term:
  - the progress terms (1.0 / 1.0);
  - mjlab's angular tracking term, unchanged;
  - Pollen's sampler, with 25% turn-in-place and VelStand's 25% standing;
  - the anchor and the warm start from b002's a02;
  - 1,500 iterations × 2,048 envs on l4x1.
- **No GPU pilot.** A CPU smoke test runs the real env with the menu's reward set (mjlab's term
  0.0, the stride term 2.0) for 2 PPO iterations and the export.

## Pass lines, written before the result (b003's, unchanged)
1. Dead band: cmd 0.10 m/s → ≥ 0.05 m/s; cmd 0.05 → ≥ 0.02.
2. Turn on the spot: wz 1.0 → ≥ 0.5 rad/s.
3. Nothing broken: falls/min ≤ 0.71; recovery within 10 points of the teacher; planar error
   ≤ 0.179.
4. cmd 0.30 m/s → ≥ 0.12 m/s.

## Expectation to beat
- **The 0.10 line is the point.** I expect it to move off zero, the first run in the series to
  do so, and give it an even chance of reaching 0.05. b003d's design expected a pass and was
  wrong. So besides the unchanged line, this time I also pre-register a weaker bar for the
  mechanism itself: the final network at cmd 0.10 ≥ 0.02 m/s on the job's 512-env speed curve
  (b003b 0.008, b003d 0.004). Below that, the mechanism did not take.
- **The 0.05 line is unlikely.** 0.05 m/s is inside the residual sway on the average.
- **0.30 m/s should come back** toward b003b's 0.120. Walking at speed now pays more than under
  mjlab's term (1.21 against 1.18 for b003b's gait at 0.30); b003d dropped to 0.073.
- Risks:
  - **Falls:** the term no longer charges sway, so a gait may rock more. b003d's falls were
    already at the line (0.714). The angular and upright terms still charge rocking.
  - **Turning:** that term is unchanged. b003c and b003d both lost b003b's 0.456.
  - **Lag:** the average trails a new command by about 0.5 s (commands hold 3–8 s), and the
    policy cannot see it.
- I'll read the 250-iteration checkpoints at 0.10 and 0.30 as for b003d, before anything else.
- **If the band still does not move,** the reward is not the whole blocker. The next change is
  the upright anchor (`coef_walk: 0`), whose pull sits on both sides of the band.
