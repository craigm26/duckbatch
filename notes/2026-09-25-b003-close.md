## Why: the lever was pulled the wrong way
mjlab's tracking terms are `exp(-error² / std²)`. b003 cut std from 0.316 to 0.1 (linear) and
from 0.707 to 0.3 (yaw), reasoning that a lenient kernel pays ~90% for standing still at
0.1 m/s. That's true, but with the tight kernel, a duck told to go 0.3 m/s earns exp(−4) ≈ 0.018
for managing 0.1 m/s and exp(−9) ≈ 0.0001 for standing still. Partial progress pays next to
nothing, so there is no gradient toward walking, while the posture, action-rate and upright
terms, plus the teacher anchor on still frames, keep paying for standing. PPO followed the
reward: the fine-tuned policy stands more. The logged tracking reward rose (0.40 → 0.55 per
episode) only because it is dominated by near-zero commands, which standing satisfies.

## Next (b003b, to pre-register)
Keep the kernel's gradient and remove the reward for standing:
- keep std at mjlab's values, and add a **progress term**: velocity along the commanded
  direction, clipped at the command (pays linearly for partial progress, and pays zero for
  standing when |cmd| > 0);
- or a kernel curriculum: start at the default std and narrow as tracking improves;
- oversample the dead band (|cmd| 0.05–0.25 m/s, |wz| 0.3–1.0) instead of changing the reward.
A 100-iteration pilot on the laptop, speed curve only, before any HF run.
