## Where the series stands
Four reward designs, five runs (b003, b003b–e). None moved 0.10 m/s off the student's 0.008:
- tighter kernel (b003)
- progress term (b003b)
- dead-band practice (b003c)
- command-relative width (b003d)
- stride-averaged relative width (b003e)

s002 showed why mjlab's own term pays nothing to walk there: the stride's sway. b003d and b003e
were built to remove that, and the band still did not open. So the reward is no longer the most
likely blocker, which the b003e design said in advance would be the conclusion.

**b003b remains the best all-round network:** 0.30 → 0.120, 0.40 → 0.183, turning 0.456, falls
0.39. b003e is the best at turning (0.525, wz 0.5 → 0.148) and weaker at planar speed.
