# b003d design: judge linear tracking relative to the command (pre-registered 2026-10-01)

## Why
b003c showed that the dead band is not short of practice: standing is too well paid there
(`notes/2026-10-01-b003c-close.md`). A CPU rollout made for this design measured what each term
pays. It used 24 envs, 6 s, the walk profile, upright envs and the last half of each episode;
the scripts and their output are in `records/s001-slow-command-terms/`.
- **All three walkers stand at 0.10 m/s.** Teacher, student and b003b move at −0.004 to
  +0.003 m/s. Their feet are in the air 6–12% of the time, with 0.7–1.5 landings/s against
  2.6–3.3 at 0.30 m/s. They do not march in place; they stand.
- **Standing collects most of mjlab's linear tracking term at 0.10.** It pays 1.72–1.79 per
  second of the term's 2.0. Walking at half the command would pay 1.95, so starting to move is
  worth +0.14/s.
- **Walking costs about nothing in the other terms.** Walking at 0.30 minus standing at 0.10
  nets −0.03 to +0.24/s across the three. Air time (+0.70 to +1.06) pays for the roll and pitch
  that angular tracking charges (−0.32 to −0.59), for posture (−0.15 to −0.21), upright (−0.03 to
  −0.11) and action rate (−0.03 to −0.07).

So what the band lacks is pay for moving at all. b003 tried a narrower width at every command,
and the gradient at speed died with it. b003d narrows the width only below 0.4 m/s, in
proportion to the command, so every command is judged by its relative error the way mjlab
judges 0.4 m/s.

**Correction to b003, b003b and b003c.** Their menus set `rel_standing_envs: 0.05`. VelStand's
`standing_envs` curriculum sets 0.25 at every reset once the step counter is past 0, and finetune
pins the counter at the final stage. TensorBoard's `Curriculum/standing_envs` reads 0.25 at every
iteration of b003b and b003c, and a live env reads 0.25. All three ran with a quarter of the envs
drawn standing. Turn-in-place then takes 25% and un-marks them, which leaves about 19% standing.
b003d keeps 0.25 and its menu says so, so the comparison with b003b is still one change.

## Method (the one change from b003b)
- **The term:** `src/duckbatch/rewards.py` `track_linear_velocity_relative` is mjlab's
  `track_linear_velocity` with the planar width multiplied by clamp(|cmd| / 0.4, 0.05 / 0.4, 1).
  - At and above 0.4 m/s it is mjlab's term exactly; a test compares it with mjlab's function.
  - From 0.05 to 0.4 m/s the planar error counts relative to the command. Standing pays
    exp(−1.6) ≈ 0.20 of the weight at every command (0.90 at 0.10 before), and half the command
    pays exp(−0.4) ≈ 0.67.
  - From 0.02 to 0.05 m/s the width stops at the 0.05 m/s floor.
  - Below 0.02 m/s it uses mjlab's width, so standing envs are judged as before.
  - Vertical speed keeps mjlab's width at every command, because bobbing belongs to the gait.
- **In the menu:** mjlab's `track_linear_velocity` gets weight 0, and the relative term goes in
  at its weight (2.0) and width (√0.1).
- **Checked in a real VelStand env** (CPU, 16 envs, the student, both terms at weight 2):
  - at 0.10 m/s, where the student stands, the relative term pays 0.41/s and mjlab's 1.80/s;
  - at 0.40 m/s and at a stand command the two are identical (1.037/s and 1.856/s).
- **At 0.10 m/s:** starting to move (half the command) now earns +0.94/s instead of +0.14/s.
  Walking the command earns +1.6/s over standing instead of +0.19/s, before the progress term's
  +1.0.
- **Everything else is b003b:**
  - the progress terms (1.0 / 1.0);
  - mjlab's angular tracking term, unchanged;
  - Pollen's own sampler (no dead-band sampler), with 25% turn-in-place;
  - the anchor and the warm start from b002's a02;
  - 1,500 iterations × 2,048 envs on l4x1.
- **No GPU pilot.** A CPU smoke test runs the real VelStand env with the menu's reward set for
  2 PPO iterations and the export.

## Pass lines, written before the result (b003's, unchanged)
1. Dead band: cmd 0.10 m/s → ≥ 0.05 m/s; cmd 0.05 → ≥ 0.02.
2. Turn on the spot: wz 1.0 → ≥ 0.5 rad/s.
3. Nothing broken: falls/min ≤ 0.71; recovery within 10 points of the teacher; planar error
   ≤ 0.179.
4. cmd 0.30 m/s → ≥ 0.12 m/s.

## Expectation to beat
- **The 0.10 line is the point of the run.** I expect it to pass: moving at half the command now
  pays more than walking the whole command paid in b003b.
- **The 0.05 line is less likely.** 0.02 m/s is close to the sway of a standing duck.
- Risks:
  - **0.30 m/s:** commands under 0.4 now get a narrower width, 0.75 of mjlab's at 0.30.
    Partial progress there still pays (0.12 of 0.30 earns 0.56 of the term), so the gradient
    b003 lost is kept. b003b's 0.1199 is the number to hold.
  - **Falls:** a slow walk may be less steady than standing. The line is 0.71/min.
  - **Turning:** that term is unchanged, but b003c lost turning when it changed something
    else. b003b's 0.456 is the number to hold.
- **If the 0.10 line still does not move,** the next change is the anchor's pull toward the
  teacher's stand at ≈0 commands. The teacher also stands at 0.10. Before blaming the reward
  again, I'll read the 250-iteration checkpoints.
- **If it works for walking,** the same treatment of the angular term is next. wz 0.5 gets
  0.121 rad/s in b003b, the angular dead band.
