## What it shows
The progress term did what b003's narrowed kernel could not. Everything above the dead band
improved on the student, without costing stability:
- turning on the spot more than doubled;
- 0.4 m/s beats the teacher;
- falls are unchanged and yaw error is lower.

Below about 0.15 m/s nothing moved: 0.008 at 0.10 is the student's number. With
`min_command` 0.02, the term pays at 0.10, so a reward for going slowly was there and PPO did
not find a slow gait. Next candidates, cheapest first:
- **b003c:** oversample the dead band (|cmd| 0.05–0.25, |wz| 0.3–1.0). b003's third option,
  never tried.
- Drop the anchor's "standing still" pull near small commands (`walk_min_speed` gates walking,
  but "or ≈0" still pulls toward the teacher's stand).
- The 250-iteration checkpoints for when the turn-in-place gain arrived.
