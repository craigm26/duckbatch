# b001 close: every attempt killed at rung 0

Batch `b001-student-size` ran to its pre-registered end in 8.6 min (3.3 min of training). All six
students crossed a kill line at the first rung, after 60 iterations: about 105,000
teacher-labelled states each, on roughly 73 envs apiece. No policy was exported. The result
stands as run; nothing below re-decides it.

## What it shows

1. **A clean size ladder even at this budget.** Falls, yaw error and action gap all worsen
   monotonically as the network shrinks, apart from a01 against a02 on falls, which is within
   noise at 73 envs.
2. **Getting up distils easily; walking under pushes does not.** Every student gets up from a
   prone spawn 86 to 100% of the time (teacher 99%), even at 3,502 parameters. The gap is in
   staying up while walking and in yaw tracking (1.5 to 3.4x the teacher's error).
3. **Planar tracking is nearly there** (1.1 to 1.6x). Yaw tracking is the hard part.

## Decision models

GLiNER2.5-Decide answered all six cases in shadow: `kill` at 0.92 to 0.94 confidence,
`gap` = 2 ("material"), `still_learning` 0.59 to 0.60. It agreed with the rules on 6 of 6, but
every case was a kill, so this does not test it. Its answers did not move with severity:
the same verdict, gap level and confidence (±0.01) for 9.7% or 29.4% time down, and for 2.1 or
11.4 falls/min. That is the pattern rtlab saw with base GLiNER2 on numeric thresholds. It is a
hint, not a measurement; b002 should produce keep cases to measure against. Jev was not run (no
key yet); the stored case texts allow `duckbatch rejudge --models jev` later.

## Where the design was wrong

- **Absolute kill lines at rung 0.** I pre-registered final-quality bars and applied them to
  a 60-iteration rung. Successive halving prunes early rungs by rank; absolute bars belong on
  the last, full-budget rung. With the rung-0 kill lines as written, no student at this budget
  could survive to show what a longer one does.
- **The budget.** My expectation ("128-128 holds within noise") assumed the full 420-iteration
  schedule, which no arm reached.

Both are fixed in b002, which is pre-registered before it runs.
