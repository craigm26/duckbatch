# k002 design: a straighter LEFT kick (pre-registered 2026-10-03)

## Why
k001 made Pollen's right kick straighter (8.0° → 3.4° off line) with no loss of distance or
stability (`notes/2026-10-03-k001-close.md`). Craig asked for the left kick next. Pollen ships
`ball_kick_left.onnx` but registers only a right-foot task (`KICK_FOOT = "right"`). duckbatch now
registers the left-foot task and its backlash twin from Pollen's own factory
(`src/duckbatch/tasks.py`, `kick_foot="left"`). The support-foot sensor is confirmed flipped: the
right foot holds up a left kick.

Pollen's left kick in its own task (CPU, 32 envs, seed 2001):

| | travel | peak speed | off line | fell | support foot down |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| plain | 5.11 m | 1.36 m/s | 0.63 m (**7.0°**) | 0% | 97.4% |
| backlash | 5.30 m | 1.45 m/s | 0.67 m (7.2°) | 0% | 97.3% |

## Method
k001's method mirrored to the left foot, unchanged:
- task Duckbatch-BallKick-Left-Flat-MicroDuck, which is Pollen's reward exactly;
- plus `ball_lateral_speed` at −12;
- warm start from Pollen's `ball_kick_left.onnx` (teachers/, sha256 d6928284…);
- plain PPO with no anchor; init_std 0.2; 1000 × 2048 on l4x1.

Measured with `skill_eval.kick`: 256 envs, seeds 2001–2003, plain task and backlash twin, Pollen's
left kick beside the fine-tune.

## Pass lines (k001's, unchanged; plain task, mean over the three seeds)
1. **Straighter:** angle off line ≤ 4.0°.
2. **Not weaker:** travel ≥ 0.95 × Pollen's.
3. **Not riskier:** falls ≤ Pollen's + 0.02; support foot down ≥ Pollen's − 0.05.
4. **Installable:** Pollen's three checks pass, with the manifest's slot `kick_left`.
- **Reported, not a line:** the backlash twin.

## Expectation
The same as k001's result, since the task is the mirror image. The one new risk is the
registration: a task variant Pollen never trained on. Line 2 and the falls line would show it.

## Not claimed
Simulation only. Nothing has run on a physical duck. Craig has none yet; some testers do.
