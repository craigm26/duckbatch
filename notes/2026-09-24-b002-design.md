# b002 design (pre-registered 2026-09-24, after b001 closed, before b002 ran)

Same question, task, teacher, students, eval profiles and final gates as b001 (see
`2026-09-24-b001-design.md` and its correction). There are two changes, both from
`2026-09-24-b001-close.md`:

1. **Rung-scaled gates.** At rungs 0 and 1, an arm dies only on a catastrophic line: falls
   more than 8/min above the teacher's, more than 25% of the time down, tracking more than 3x
   the teacher's error, or recovery more than 50 points below the teacher's. Everything else
   competes on rank for the quota (ceil(n/2)). The unchanged b001 gates (`DEFAULT_GATES`)
   apply at the final rung, where they decide what counts as holding the teacher's line.
2. **2.5x budget:** rungs of 150, 300 and 600 iterations (b001: 60, 120, 240). The final
   survivors see about 1,050 iterations.

Under b001's rung-0 numbers, only a06 (32-32) would die at rung 0 under the early lines.

## Expectation to beat

- By rung 2, the two finalists are mid-size (the efficiency rank favours small arms that pass
  keep lines; at early rungs few will, so the quota goes by quality and the larger arms
  advance).
- At 1,050 iterations, a 57k or 26k student reaches the teacher's planar tracking, stays
  within 1.5x on yaw, and falls under 1/min. I expect it NOT to hold every final keep line:
  yaw is the hard part.
- Decide: if b002 produces keep cases, I expect it to call most of them `kill`, since its b001
  answers did not move with the numbers.

## Not claimed

The same limits as b001: sim only, imitation rather than RL, latency from a laptop.
