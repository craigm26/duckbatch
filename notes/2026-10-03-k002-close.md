## Verdict
**k002 fails.** The same method that took the right kick from 8.0° to 3.4° took the left kick
from 7.2° to 5.0°: a real improvement (−30%, against k001's −58%), consistent across all three
seeds and under backlash, but short of the line set before the run. It is not offered as a better
left kick.

## What it shows
- The left-foot task variant trains cleanly. Distance and falls are unchanged, so the
  registration (the one new risk in the design) was not the problem.
- The mirror image did not respond equally. The left kick starts 0.8° straighter than the right
  and ends 1.6° less straight. One reading is that −12 on sideways ball speed is not enough for
  this network: the forward term still pays more for a slightly-angled hard kick than the side
  term charges. That is a hypothesis for k002b, not a finding.
- Support foot down dropped 1.3 points (still well inside the line). k001 dropped 0.2.
