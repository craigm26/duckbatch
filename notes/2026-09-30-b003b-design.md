# b003b design: pay for going, not for standing (pre-registered 2026-09-30)

## Why

b003 narrowed mjlab's tracking widths so standing at a slow command would stop paying ~90%.
It failed every pass line but stability (`notes/2026-09-25-b003-close.md`). With the tight
kernel, a duck commanded 0.3 m/s earns exp(−4) ≈ 0.018 for managing 0.1 m/s. Partial progress
paid nothing, so there was no gradient toward walking, and PPO followed the posture, upright
and anchor terms into standing more.

## Method (the one change from b003)

- **Tracking widths back to mjlab's own** (linear √0.1, angular √0.5). The `rewards:` overrides
  are removed.
- **Two new terms** (`src/duckbatch/rewards.py`), added through the menu's new `add_rewards`:
  - `command_progress_linear` (weight 1.0): speed along the commanded planar direction, clipped
    to [0, |cmd|], divided by |cmd|. It pays 0 below 0.02 m/s commanded.
  - `command_progress_angular` (weight 1.0): the same for yaw rate. It pays 0 below 0.1 rad/s
    commanded.
  Standing when told to move now earns 0 from these, and half the commanded speed earns 0.5.
  The tracking terms (weight 2.0 each) still pay for accuracy.
- **Everything else as b003:**
  - warm start from b002's a02 (61-128-128-14);
  - init_std 0.25, lr 3e-4, entropy 0.002;
  - the same teacher anchor;
  - command mix of 25% turn-in-place and 5% standing;
  - 1500 iterations × 2048 envs on HF Jobs l4x1.
- **Pilot first:** `menus/b003b-pilot.yaml`, 100 iterations, the same everything. It checks
  the term runs without NaNs and that the logged progress reward rises. It is not judged
  against the pass lines.

## Pass lines, written before the result (b003's, unchanged, so the two are comparable)

1. **Dead band gone:** at 0.10 m/s commanded, ≥ 0.05 m/s; at 0.05 m/s, ≥ 0.02 m/s.
2. **Turns on the spot:** at 1.0 rad/s commanded with no linear command, ≥ 0.5 rad/s.
3. **Nothing broken:**
   - falls/min ≤ 0.71 (the student's 0.46 + 0.25);
   - recovery within 10 points of the teacher's;
   - planar tracking error no worse than the student's (0.179).
4. **At 0.3 m/s commanded,** ≥ 0.12 m/s.

## Expectation to beat

Pass 4 and pass 1's 0.10 line by iteration 1500. Linear progress gives exactly the gradient b003
lacked. Pass 2 is less likely: turning in place is a different gait from the student's. Risk:
progress pays for lunging, so falls/min may rise. The anchor on fallen frames is the guard.

## Not claimed

Simulation only, in mjlab. This is PPO fine-tuning of Pollen's work and duckbatch's distillation,
not a new gait. A pass is not a claim about the physical duck.
