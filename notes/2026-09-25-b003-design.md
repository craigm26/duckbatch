# b003 design: teach the student to walk slowly and turn on the spot (pre-registered 2026-09-25)

## Why

The arena and the router exposed a hole. "Walk slowly" is a step the router proposes, and the
duck cannot do it. In mjlab (`records/s000-speed-curve`, measured before this batch), Pollen's
velstand answers commands of 0.05 and 0.10 m/s with 0.001 to 0.002 m/s, which is standing still,
and reaches only 0.12 m/s when commanded 0.3. Its distilled students inherited that. In
duckbench's plain-MuJoCo scene, neither the teacher nor the students turn on the spot.

The cause is the reward, not the command sampling. VelStand already samples small commands and
turns in place (15% of envs). Its tracking reward has a width of √0.1 ≈ 0.32 m/s, so standing
still when asked for 0.1 m/s earns about 90% of it.

## Method

- **Warm start:** PPO starting from the b002 128-128 student (26,254 params). The weights and a
  frozen normaliser are loaded so the actor computes exactly what the ONNX does. Initial
  exploration std is 0.25, learning rate 3e-4, entropy 0.002. The critic is fresh (512-256-128).
- **Reward:** narrower tracking widths (linear 0.1, angular 0.3). Turn-in-place envs go from 15%
  to 25%, and standing envs are 5%.
- **Teacher anchor** (duckbatch `PpoWithTeacherAnchor`, the pattern of microduck_rl's
  `PpoWithExpertBc`, with the expert being Pollen's published velstand.onnx):
  - behaviour-cloned to the teacher on fallen frames (weight 1.0), so recovery is not
    unlearned;
  - a light pull (0.2) when upright and commanded 0.35 m/s or more, or standing still;
  - the dead band (slow walking, turning in place) is left to PPO alone.
- **Budget:** 1500 iterations × 2048 envs on Hugging Face Jobs (l4x1), launched with
  `duckbatch hf-job menus/b003-dead-band.yaml --run finetune`.
- **Measured after:** the speed-versus-command curve (mjlab, walk profile, 512 envs, 8 s, the
  last half of each episode) for the teacher, the student and the fine-tuned student. Then
  b002's final evaluation (walk 30 s + recovery 6 s, seeds 2001 to 2003, teacher in the same
  runs). The arena follows once it is published.

## Pass lines, written before the result

1. **Dead band gone:** at 0.10 m/s commanded, the fine-tuned student moves at 0.05 m/s or more;
   at 0.05 m/s, at 0.02 m/s or more.
2. **Turns on the spot:** at 1.0 rad/s commanded with no linear command, a yaw rate of 0.5 rad/s
   or more.
3. **Nothing broken:**
   - falls/min no worse than the student's b002 figure (0.46) + 0.25;
   - recovery within 10 points of the teacher's;
   - planar tracking error no worse than the student's, averaged over the eval's command mix.
4. **Tracking at 0.3 m/s commanded** at least the teacher's 0.12 m/s.

## Expectation to beat

Passes 1 and 2 by iteration 1500. The narrowed width is a strong signal, and the student already
has the gait. Pass 3 is the risk: a tighter reward and a free dead band may cost stability.
Falls/min may rise, which would be exactly why the anchor exists.

## Not claimed

Simulation only. RL here is fine-tuning of Pollen's work and duckbatch's distillation, not a new
gait from scratch.
