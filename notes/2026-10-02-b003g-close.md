## Verdict
By the letter of the pre-registered lines, b003g passes 7 of 8. It is the first run in the
series to pass the dead-band lines, and it has the best falls of any fine-tune. By what the lines
were for, a duck that walks slowly when told to, it does not. The slow forward speed comes with
a constant clockwise turn and no ability to stand still. **This network should not go into
Behaviours as a slow walker.** The lines measured forward speed only, which is the gap. Future
runs in this series add two lines: yaw on straight commands |wz| ≤ 0.05, and stand drift at
cmd 0 |v| ≤ 0.05.

What it does show is that **standing practice was holding the band shut.** It is the first
change in six that moved cmd 0.10 at all. The next run has to keep that and get standing and
straightness back.

## Next (not run; each needs a go-ahead)

   algorithm config, along with `bc_cfg`. Pollen trains velstand with left/right symmetry

   steady one-way yaw bias is what asymmetry looks like. b003h = b003g + symmetry restored. This
   is a hypothesis about the cause, not a finding.
