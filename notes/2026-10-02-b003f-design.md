# b003f design: let go of the teacher while upright (pre-registered 2026-10-02)

## Why
Five runs (b003, b003b–e) changed the reward four ways, and none moved cmd 0.10 m/s off the
student's 0.008. b003d and b003e removed the stride-sway cause that s002 measured, and still no
checkpoint ever walked at 0.10. Both close notes named the same next suspect.

The anchor pulls the actor toward Pollen's velstand on upright frames commanded ≈0, or at
≥ 0.35 m/s (`walk_min_speed`). Those are both sides of the band, and velstand itself stands
still at 0.10 (0.002 m/s). So every PPO update that found a slow step was followed by a
behaviour-cloning pass toward a network that has no slow step on either side.

## Method (the one change from b003b)
- `anchor.coef_walk: 0.0`, from 0.2. Fallen frames keep `coef_fallen: 1.0` with the same
  averaging as b003b, so the recovery pull is unchanged.
- Everything else is b003b's (`menus/b003b-progress.yaml`):
  - mjlab's tracking terms at their own widths, plus the two progress terms (1.0 / 1.0);
  - warm start from b002's a02;
  - init_std 0.25, lr 3e-4, entropy 0.002;
  - 1,500 iterations × 2,048 envs on HF Jobs l4x1.
- **Built on b003b, not b003e.** b003b is the best all-round network, and one change against
  it is readable. b003e's stride term can be combined later if this moves the band.
- **Still not changed:** standing envs remain at VelStand's curriculum 25% (see the b003d
  close). That is the next change if this one does not move the band.
- No pilot and no new code: a single number in a menu that b003b already ran.

## Pass lines, written before the result (b003's, unchanged)
1. Dead band: cmd 0.10 m/s → ≥ 0.05 m/s; cmd 0.05 → ≥ 0.02.
2. Turn on the spot: wz 1.0 → ≥ 0.5 rad/s.
3. Nothing broken: falls/min ≤ 0.71; recovery within 10 points of the teacher; planar error
   ≤ 0.179.
4. cmd 0.30 m/s → ≥ 0.12 m/s.
- M. Mechanism bar (as b003e): the final network at cmd 0.10 ≥ 0.02 m/s on the 512-env curve.
  Below that, letting go of the teacher did not open the band.

## Expectation to beat
- **I give the mechanism bar about one in three.** The anchor sits on both sides of the band,
  but b003c showed standing is also well paid by the reward at small commands, and the
  standing curriculum still makes a quarter of the practice "do not move".
- **Risks:**
  - **Gait quality:** with no upright pull, the policy may drift from velstand's gait at speed.
    Falls and planar error are the lines that would show it.
  - **Turning** is free to go either way. b003b's 0.456 is the reference.
- **Checkpoints first:** read at 0.10 and 0.30 before the close note, as for b003d and b003e.
