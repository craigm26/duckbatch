## What it shows
One reward term that charges sideways ball speed, weighted like the forward term, halved the
kick's error in 1,000 iterations and cost no distance and no stability. It holds under backlash,
which the policy never trained on, so it is not a fit to the plain actuator.

Peak ball speed fell slightly (1.34 → 1.28 m/s). No line covered it. Travel is unchanged, so the
straighter kick spends less of its speed going sideways.
