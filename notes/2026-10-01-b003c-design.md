# b003c design: practise the dead band (pre-registered 2026-10-01)

## Why
b003b's progress term fixed turning (wz 1.0 → 0.456 rad/s, from the student's 0.209) and fast
walking (0.4 m/s → 0.183 m/s), but it left the dead band alone: 0.008 m/s at a 0.10 m/s command,
the student's own number. A reward for going slowly was in place and PPO found no slow gait.
VelStand samples planar commands uniformly in ±0.4 × ±0.3 m/s, so only about 15% land under
0.15 m/s.

## Method (the one change from b003b)
- `src/duckbatch/commands.py` `DeadBandCommandCfg` replaces Pollen's twist command. Every
  upstream field is carried over: ranges, standing envs, its own turn-in-place practice at 25%.
- Then 35% of the envs that are neither standing nor turning in place get a planar speed
  uniform in [0.05, 0.25] m/s, direction uniform on the circle, no yaw. This is the pattern
  Pollen's `VelocityCommandCommandOnly` uses for turning in place. The class is identical at
  microduck_rl `cb70b79`, which the job runs, and `cfe1c2a`, which the tests ran against.
- Everything else is b003b: the progress terms (weights 1.0 / 1.0), mjlab's tracking widths,
  the warm start from b002's a02, the same anchor, and 1,500 iterations × 2,048 envs on l4x1.
- No GPU pilot. b003b's pilot showed the recipe runs. The new code (the sampler inside a real
  VelStand env, PPO steps and export) was smoke-tested on CPU first.

## Pass lines, written before the result (b003's, unchanged)
1. Dead band: cmd 0.10 m/s → ≥ 0.05 m/s; cmd 0.05 → ≥ 0.02.
2. Turn on the spot: wz 1.0 → ≥ 0.5 rad/s.
3. Nothing broken: falls/min ≤ 0.71; recovery within 10 points of the teacher; planar error
   ≤ 0.179.
4. cmd 0.30 m/s → ≥ 0.12 m/s.

## Expectation to beat
The 0.10 line in pass 1 is the point of the run, and the expectation is that it moves well off
0.008 even if it misses 0.05. Risks:
- **Turning and fast walking may slip:** about a quarter of the practice moves into the band.
  b003b's 0.456 and 0.1199 are the numbers to hold.
- **The anchor's "standing still" pull** (`walk_min_speed` "or ≈0") is untouched, and may still
  favour standing at the smallest commands. It is the next change if the band does not move.
