## What it shows
- **The anchor was holding turning back.** With the upright pull gone, turn-in-place went
  0.456 → 0.806 on the same reward. velstand's turning was the ceiling the anchor kept pulling
  toward.
- **It was not holding the dead band shut.** Six runs now, five interventions:
  - a tighter kernel (b003)
  - a progress term (b003b)
  - band practice (b003c)
  - a relative width (b003d)
  - a stride average (b003e)
  - no upright anchor (b003f)
  
  None moved cmd 0.10 off the student's ≈0.008.
