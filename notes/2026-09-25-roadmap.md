# Roadmap: from words to smooth sequences (2026-09-25)

Craig's goal is to say something in plain language and have the duck do a smooth sequence of
skills, trained on a small GPU and built with any LLM, VLA or other technique that helps,
across Microduck, OpenCastor and Gemma plan-tree. This note fixes the order and why, before
any step has a result. Each step gets its own pre-registered design and close.

## Order

1. **Make the words executable (b003).** "Walk slowly" and "turn left on the spot" are steps
   the router proposes that the walkers cannot do: velstand stands still at 0.05 and 0.10 m/s
   and turns at 0.08 rad/s when asked for 0.5, in mjlab (s000). Fix: a PPO fine-tune of the
   distilled student, with the reward narrowed and the teacher kept as an anchor where it is
   good. Runs on Hugging Face Jobs (`duckbatch hf-job … --run finetune`).
2. **One student, many skills (b004).** Pollen ships walk, stand, sit/stand, ground pick and the
   kicks as separate networks, and a sequence swaps networks mid-motion. One small student
   distilled from all of them, told the skill in the observation's unused command slots, and
   trained through random switches, turns transitions into something learned. This is measured
   against network swapping (Pollen's runtime) on falls around switches and settle time.
3. **A planner that proposes, then checks (p002).**
   - Gemma 3 (local, llama.cpp, JSON-schema-constrained to the skill vocabulary) proposes
     candidate sequences, with order, repeats and simple conditions.
   - Candidate plans run as arms in one simulator batch (duckbatch's population), and fall-free
     candidates that do what was asked survive.
   - A decision model triages the borderline ones. This is Craig's Gemma plan-tree, with
     batched simulation as the verifier.
4. **Grounding and mimicry.**
   - **The iPhone as the duck's eyes (Craig, 2026-09-25).** The phone's camera and an on-device
     vision model (Apple Vision/Core ML detectors, ARKit scene understanding, or a small VLM such
     as Gemma 3's vision projector) turn "go to the ball" into a target (bearing, range) that the
     step 1-2 skills execute. Duck Studio already runs ARKit and places the duck in the room.
   - **Mimicry:** video → pose keypoints → reference motion → mjlab's motion-tracking task
     (`tasks/tracking`), which trains on a small GPU with fewer envs.

## Across all of it

- **The arena is the bench:** a "play a plan" mode runs any sequence against every policy, and
  people's choices feed `duck-feedback/0`.
- **Formats stay portable:** `duck-intent-plan` grows into a sequence format that could map
  onto OpenCastor's RCAN messages (to be checked against RCAN before promising).
