Microduck walker arena

Every valid walking policy, one set of controls. Pollen's teacher walks beside the smaller students distilled from it by duckbatch. Each duck has its own physics world, reset identically, so the only difference between lanes is the network.

Buttons: tap to set a direction, tap again or ■ to stop · keys (hold): ↑↓ or W S forward, back · ←→ or A D turn · P push · R reset · Space pause · gamepad: left stick, B push, X reset

Standing still is a result. Pollen's velstand and the students distilled from it were trained to stand below a command threshold. In this scene velstand stands below about 0.3 m/s and the 128-128 student below about 0.4, and neither turns on the spot, while alpha_walking does. Full speed is 0.4 m/s, the top of their training range.

Per lane, since the last reset: falls (tilted past 60°), velocity-tracking error while upright, jitter (mean change in action per tick), and distance. The same definitions as duckbatch's walk evaluation.

What this is not. The scene is plain MuJoCo, not the mjlab training environment, so it is a transfer test as much as a comparison. Every walker covers less ground here than it is asked to — duckbench's own walk test measures Pollen's alpha_walking at 0.11 m/s when commanded 0.2 — and the gap is the same scene for every lane. The measured comparison is in duckbatch's b002 and p001 notes. Nothing here is a real robot.
