Results page for [craigm26/duckbatch](https://github.com/craigm26/duckbatch): Pollen Robotics'
Microduck walker distilled into much smaller students on one 4 GB laptop GPU, every attempt
judged by pre-registered rules, with decision models (Jev, GLiNER2.5-Decide) measured as judges.
Every finalist can be driven in
[Pollen's simulator](https://huggingface.co/spaces/pollen-robotics/microduck-simulator).

Simulation only. `data.json` is generated by `scripts/build_space_data.py` from `records/`.
